The project

One assistant. Many useful workflows.

J.A.R.V.I.S. (Just A Rather Very Intelligent System) is an independent project led by Manjeet Kumar in India. The goal is to create a practical assistant that can support both personal productivity and office operations.

Project principles

Useful by design, privacy-conscious, security-first, transparent about what is implemented, and built to work across supported devices wherever practical.

Capabilities in the vision

Designed around real-world work

The following areas describe the project's intended capabilities. Availability and maturity may vary while development continues.

✦

AI assistance

Conversational help for questions, research, planning, and everyday tasks using supported AI models.

◉

Voice interaction

A voice-led experience with a focus on natural interaction and Hindi-language usability.

▤

Office productivity

Planned workflows for documents, tasks, reminders, meetings, and operational coordination.

⌘

Connected AI tools

A modular approach to AI providers and tools, with reliability and fallback behavior as design goals.

▣

Personal workspace

A unified interface intended to make useful information and workflows easier to access.

⌁

Device flexibility

A web-first direction for access across supported Android devices and computers.

Development roadmap

Building carefully, step by step

These are development priorities, not claims that every item is already available.

1
Core assistant experience
Continue improving the working application, interface, and core AI workflows.
2
Reliable AI integration
Improve provider integration, model selection, error handling, and fallback design.
3
Voice and language
Test speech input and output on target devices and improve Hindi support.
4
Security and privacy
Strengthen authentication, access control, data handling, and transparent privacy practices.
5
Office workflows
Develop and validate document, task, reminder, and meeting workflows.
6
Public product release
Prepare documentation, support channels, and a sustainable free and premium model.
Trust and responsibility

Security and privacy matter

J.A.R.V.I.S. is being developed with privacy and responsible handling of user information as priorities. Specific safeguards will be documented as they are implemented and tested.

Our commitments

We aim to use clear permissions, protect credentials, apply access controls, minimize unnecessary data collection, and communicate limitations honestly. No claim of independent security certification is made.

Sustainable product vision

Free to get started. More capability over time.

The proposed business model is a free basic experience with optional paid plans for advanced capabilities in the future. Pricing, availability, and plan details have not yet been announced.
